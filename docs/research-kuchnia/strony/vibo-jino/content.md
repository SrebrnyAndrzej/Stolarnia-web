# Jino | VVR

Jino is the new Vibo's patented concept of pull-out unit, allowing you to have everything you need within easy reach.

It can be fully extracted from the cabinet, becoming an actual trolley. The comfortable handle and the front omnidirectional wheels make its movement practical and smooth in the kitchen or any other area where it is used. After use, the wheels, designed to fold inward, allow the pull-out unit to be easily pushed back into the cabinet, transforming it into a practical and efficient storage solution.

[](https://www.viboitaly.com/en/products/jino-epoxy-coated-green-jino60vvr#collapse-desc)

Features and benefits

* * *

  * Pull-out trolley designed for 60 cm cabinet base/column
  * HPL surfaces
  * Upper storage and support compartment
  * Two openable side panels
  * Drawer with soft-closing system
  * Multipurpose tray
  * Bottle holder organizers
  * Compartment with winery accessories
  * Omnidirectional front wheels
  * Handle for an easy extraction
  * Total extension slides and soft-closing system

# TANDEM – larders designed like refrigerators!

When the Kesseböhmer TANDEM door is opened, the contents are divided into door shelf cabinet shelf. Thanks to intelligent pull‐out technology , the rear shelves automatically pull‐out technology out during the opening process. This creates an impressive amount of overview and optimal accessibility to supplies. Together with the door shelf , this door shelf maximum storage space overview. 

### All product advantages at a glance

  * All storage items directly accessible storage items just one hand movement
  * Easy height adjustment of the levels at any time
  * Perfect storage center in combination with refrigerator
  * Each pull‐out shelf hold up to 60 kg. 
  * Each door shelf 24 kg
  * eTouch for handle-free, quiet, and fully automatic opening with light pressure—can also be retrofitted
  * SoftSTOPP for soft closing of the pantry door
  * Widths from 450 to 600 mm
  * Heights from 1100 to 1700 mm

### Spacious pantry

With a footprint of 60x50 cm, it can hold more than a week's worth of dry food for a family of four. Together with the refrigerator , TANDEM forms the storage center in the kitchen. With its high load capacity of 60 kg in the pull-out frame and 24 kg in the door shelf , TANDEM can be loaded with anything that does not require refrigeration without any worries.

[](https://www.kesseboehmer.com/en/storage-solutions/kitchen/larder-units/tandem-family/tandem)

### Individual height adjustment

Adjusting the height of the shelves inside the unit and on the door provides flexibility whenever you need it. The fitting elements can be hung or removed without tools with a single movement of your hand.

[View brochure](https://kesseboehmer.canto.de/v/stauraumloesungen/album/RSKPN?display=curatedView&viewIndex=0&gSortingForward=false&gOrderProp=uploadDate&referenceTo=&from=fitView)

### YouboXx maximum organization in door shelf

The YouboXx makes interior organization in the TANDEM side particularly flexible. Seven matching sets for thedoor shelf a customized organization system for every cabinet width application. All YouboXxes—just like the shelves—can be adjusted in height and are simplyhooked in the support runner . For optimal use of storage space and virtually unlimited combination options.

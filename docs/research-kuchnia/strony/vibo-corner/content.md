# Pull-out Smart Corner

The pull-out Smart Corner makes any corner cabinet "smart." Consisting of a pair of main baskets that come out simultaneously with the cabinet door and a pair of sliding inner baskets that can be pulled out completely, it improves the usability while optimizing the corner and minimizing the waste of space. The possibility to customize across different ranges makes it particularly versatile. 

Features and benefits

* * *

  * Pull-out base corner mechanism for 45-50 cm cabinet door
  * Two door baskets come out simultaneously with the cabinet door 
  * Two sliding inner baskets that can be pulled out completely
  * Baskets with sheet metal bottom and edge
  * Total extension slides with soft-closing system
  * Non-reversible mechanism

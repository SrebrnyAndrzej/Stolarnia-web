### All product advantages at a glance

  * Both shelf levels move independently of each other
  * height adjustment shelves: flexibility and maximum use of space
  * Up to 70% more storage space compared to a cabinet pull-outs next to the "dead" corner!
  * Each tray hold up to 25 kg.
  * Quick, secure, toolless mounting ClickFixx
  * Soft-close self-closing mechanism (optional)
  * The opening angle limiter prevents damage to the fronts of adjacent components.

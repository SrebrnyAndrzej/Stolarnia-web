### 807

|   
---  
[](https://www.camar.it/modulo_rich_info.php)The new **patented 807 hanging system** is a revolution for the world of suspended base cabinets. Made totally in **metal** , it has a **thickness of only 10mm** , and is able to support very heavy loads with a guarantee of safety. Tested at **TÜV LGA** according to European norms EN14749, EN14688 and ISO 7170, it has a declared **loading capacity of 120 kg** , which is stamped on each piece. **Very easy to fit** , through **4 x 10mm diameter holes** in the side panel of the cabinet, where the 4 dowels are inserted, it has an **adjustment range of 19mm horizontally and 22mm vertically**. Uniquely compatible with the new **897 series wall plates** , with an innovative and revolutionary **anti-drop system** which is activated **automatically** when the bracket is hooked into the wall plate and prevents the cabinet from accidentally falling from the wall. The system is completed by a compatible **cover** in plastic, available in several colours and sizes, according to the position of the rear panel in the cabinet.

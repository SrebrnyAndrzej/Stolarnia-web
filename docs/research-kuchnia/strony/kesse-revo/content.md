# REVO 90°\- the convenient corner unit solution for revolving front panels

The corner unit is a storage miracle in the kitchen A light pull on the handle is enough to set the kitchen carousel in motion. It can rotate in both directions, with the front wings moving cabinet through the cabinet . After one rotation, the rotation automatically slows down and the fronts close gently again. Thanks to the non-slip coating on the shelves, everything stays in place.

### All product advantages at a glance

  * Shelves are step- and toolless height‐adjustable
  * Optimal front stability thanks to patented door connections
  * For 80 and 90 cm corner cabinets and different cabinet heights
  * Rotation possible in both directions
  * Load capacity of 25 kg per tray
  * Two shelves as standard, optionally expandable by a third
  * Axleheight‐adjustable toolless
  * Quick, secure, toolless mounting ClickFixx
  * The closing speed can be toolless thanks to REVOfit.

### High load capacity

The high load capacity of 25 kg per shelf makes the kitchen corner base unit a true storage miracle. As much as 57 kg can be stored in the unit on up to three levels.

[](https://www.kesseboehmer.com/en/storage-solutions/kitchen/corner-units/revo-90)

### Infinite height adjustment

Just like the shelves, the spindle is height-adjustable, which is why the REVO 90 can be installed in a kitchen corner unit with a clear height of 660 – 860 mm. The overall height adjustment ensures the best use of space. Which means there’s even room for tall pans and food processors.
